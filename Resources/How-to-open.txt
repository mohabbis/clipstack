How to open Clipstack

macOS shows “Clipstack” Not Opened the first time you open this download.
Apple says it could not verify Clipstack is free of malware. That dialog
appears because this build is not notarized yet. The app does not launch
from it.

1. Unzip Clipstack.zip.
2. Drag Clipstack.app into your Applications folder.
3. Double-click Clipstack. When macOS shows “Clipstack” Not Opened, click
   Done. Move to Trash deletes the app you just downloaded.
4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to Clipstack.
6. Enter your password. When macOS asks if you're sure, click Open.

You only do this once.

On macOS 14 Sonoma, you can Control-click Clipstack.app and choose Open.
On macOS 15 and later, use Privacy & Security as above.

Clipstack has no Dock icon. After it opens, click the clipboard icon in
the menu bar, or press Control-Option-Command-V.
